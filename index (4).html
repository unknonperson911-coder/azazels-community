📌  A Message to the FamilyPinned

THE AZAZELS THE LAWS OF THE REALM Strength earns respect. Loyalty earns a place. Discipline keeps the realm standing. I. — RESPECT THE REALM Treat every member with respect. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and unnecessary toxicity are not tolerated. II. — HONOR YOUR COMMITMENTS If you sign up for Alliance War, Alliance Quest, or another alliance activity, participate responsibly. Inform leadership if something prevents you from completing your assigned duties. III. — WAR DISCIPLINE Follow assigned paths, tactics, and leadership instructions. Communicate before making major changes to your assignment. Repeated negligence may result in a review of your position. IV. — COMMUNICATION IS MANDATORY Read official announcements and check important updates regularly. If an officer asks for confirmation, respond within the stated deadline. V. — ACTIVITY & INACTIVITY Stay active according to the alliance's expectations. If you need a break, inform leadership in advance whenever possible. Extended unexplained inactivity may result in removal. VI. — RESPECT THE CHAIN OF COMMAND Officers are responsible for coordination and fair enforcement. You may question a decision respectfully, but do not disrupt alliance activities over disagreements. VII. — CONSEQUENCES Rule violations may lead to a warning, temporary restrictions, removal from an activity, or removal from the alliance, depending on severity and repeated behavior. Serious violations may require immediate action. ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ ♛ THE AZAZELS CODE Stay loyal. Stay active. Respect the realm. Grow stronger together. One alliance. One standard. No unnecessary chaos

Our principles:

  • Respect every member
  • Stay active and participate
  • Keep improving together

— The Azazels Council
